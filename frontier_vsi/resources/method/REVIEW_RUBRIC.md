# Review Rubric

Chapter diagnostic score uses six orthogonal dimensions: facts/evidence 25, argument/structure 20, explanation/insight 20, author judgement/selection 15, readability 10, language/voice 10. Authorial Presence remains a separate PASS/FAIL gate. Any CRITICAL factual error, fabricated citation, false precision, unsupported exact quotation or claim materially stronger than its evidence is an automatic failure. Revision is bounded; repeated failure routes to the responsible upstream artifact.
